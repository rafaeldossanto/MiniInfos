# Faixas de Atena

Artes aprovadas para o cabeçalho dos temas Clássico escuro e Clássico claro, geradas com a ferramenta integrada `image_gen`. Os PNGs foram reduzidos proporcionalmente para 1200 × 400, sem recortar ou alterar a composição. A landing page usa versões WebP das mesmas artes, em `front/public/art/widget-header-dark.webp` e `widget-header-light.webp`.

- `athena-banner-dark.png`: gravura, disco e oliveiras nas duas laterais.
- `athena-banner-light.png`: a mesma composição com Atena em mármore claro.

As estátuas grandes da landing page continuam usando suas artes anteriores. As referências fornecidas foram os recortes de cabeçalho do usuário e a prévia aprovada dos dois cartões. Não há textos, controles ou marcas de fornecedores nas faixas.

## Prompt da gravura

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: production header banner artwork for MiniInfos desktop widget.
Reference image 1 is the exact approved subject/composition; reference image 2 is the approved app preview, provided only to see the wide Athena band. Produce ONLY the painting/engraving from inside the horizontal band. Do not include interface, borders, round corners, text, owl icon, buttons, numbers, callouts or labels.
A beautiful close portrait of the Greek goddess Athena with ornate crested helmet, tilted face looking down and to the right, detailed circular engraved architectural disc directly behind her head, balanced olive branches at BOTH far sides. Keep Athena's face exactly in the horizontal center of the full composition. The close crop intentionally cuts off the helmet top and bust at lower edge like the reference. Wide horizontal landscape banner, about 3:1 aspect ratio, at least 1536 pixels wide. Fine antique copperplate engraving with delicate warm ivory/silver lines on dark charcoal black stone, detailed expressive classical female face. Full-bleed artwork edge to edge, dark graphite backdrop, restrained contrast, monochrome and warm stone accents, no orange or gold. Balanced composition must read well in single and split card widths. No UI or text, no logos, no gradients or white margins.

## Prompt do mármore

Use case: style-transfer. Asset type: production MiniInfos widget horizontal header banner.
EDIT TARGET: image 1 is the final dark engraving banner. Reference image 2 shows the approved LIGHT rendering inside the small band only.
Change ONLY the material/rendering of Athena to warm white ivory MARBLE, softly gallery-lit, with sculpted stone facial planes and exquisitely carved helmet. Keep the EXACT 3:1 canvas, composition, placement of the face, helmet, hair, circular engraved disc, olive branches on BOTH sides, crop and all outlines. Athena remains centered. Preserve the dark charcoal/graphite backdrop and architectural disc, and pale silver olive branches, so the marble head is bright and stands out like the light reference. No UI, no text, no icons, no logos, no borders or round corners, no white margins. This is a full-bleed raster banner asset, not an app screenshot. Match source registration exactly for a smooth theme crossfade.
