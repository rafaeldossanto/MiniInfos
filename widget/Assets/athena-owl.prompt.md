# Coruja de Atena

Ferramenta: `image_gen`, modo integrado. Arte original gerada para o MiniInfos.

Arquivo usado pelo aplicativo: `athena-owl.png`, 1254 × 1254, PNG com transparência real. Quatro quadros para cada estado: repouso, atividade, atenção e sono. O aplicativo recorta e alinha os quadros em memória; o arquivo gerado não foi modificado.

Prompt final de refinamento, usando a primeira geração como referência:

> Edit this owl sprite sheet for production quality. Preserve the same cute ivory and bronze Athena owl identity, same 4 columns and 4 rows, and same animation states (row1 idle blink, row2 wingflap, row3 attentive head tilt, row4 sleep). Critical fixes: EVERY owl must be much SMALLER within its own cell, around 58% of the cell height with 20% empty transparent margin ABOVE and BELOW. Top row helmets MUST NOT touch the canvas edge. All 16 owl sprites, including the widest spread wings, must be fully inside each equal square cell and cannot touch neighboring cells. All feet sit at the same local baseline in each cell. Align the 4x4 grid with mathematical regularity. All character scale remains constant across rows.
>
> Remove ALL red and green fringes/outlines/noise around the sprites. Use only cream, ivory, warm gray, muted bronze and deep brown; NO red, NO green anywhere, including the helmet crest or leaves. Helmet ornament can be bronze monochrome. Clean strong dark brown silhouette edges with transparent pixels directly outside, no chroma-key colors, no glows, no semi-transparent colored halo. True alpha transparent background, not black or white or a painted checkerboard. Clean pixel-art sprite sheet asset, not a UI. No labels, borders, grid lines or text. Keep the entire square canvas and generous transparent padding.
